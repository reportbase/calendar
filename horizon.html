Walk out. Press and drag to the right: the gold needle is a speed, and the blue walks out along the road. Drag left to come back. Held at 45° it walks one "one in two" every 1.7 s; nearer the horizon, faster. It never gets there.

The thumb. Wheel, pinch, ↑ ↓ or thumb − + change H. The needle stays; the posts slide under it, and half the cells always lie within one thumb.

What you see. The outer ring is the quarter turn in 256 cells, each painted by the first post it meets. The heat band says how much turn each post gets: all of it at home, half at the corner, none at the horizon.

The writer's count. How many posts each cell covers: at most one near home, more past the corner, and without end in the last. The reader's cells are the same whatever the count.
