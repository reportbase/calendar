Move. Press anywhere on the dial and drag sideways: the gold needle is a speed, the farther from upright the faster the blue moves. Let go and the window rests on the record reached. ← → do the same.

Window. Wheel, pinch, ↑ ↓ or − + make the window wider or narrower: the date's fields in teal are within one unit of the needle, blue within the window, gold out of reach.

Heat. The shaded arc is the turn's heat, cos²θ: near upright a small turn moves one unit; at 45° (the corner) half as finely; near the horizon a hair of turn crosses the whole window.

Jump. now, centre, or go to a date. Figure. Drag P, T, H or S; the wheel over it zooms it; [ ] the shape’s breadth; hide it with figure (f) for a dial the size of the screen.
