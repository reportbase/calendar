the road
Every sleeper is a record of the dial's list, and the country either side is made out of the addresses themselves — nothing is stored.
The road rolls toward you at a walking pace. The space bar does the same, a double tap on the road starts it, and a hand on the road stops it.
A counter, for finding flicker. Each canvas reports how many times a second it CLEARED AND REPAINTED itself. A still page should read 0 · 0 · 0. If it reads 0 and the page is flickering anyway, the flicker is not this page repainting — and the line beneath names the last thing that changed, which says who is asking for the repaints when they happen.
The record standing at each sleeper, written beside it wherever there is room, drawn over everything and the dial included. Off to begin with: the street is built up instead, one building to a cell, and the names take the ground again when this is on.
