Where you stand. One thumb above the map, looking down. The middle is straight down; the rim is the horizon all round, the whole world crowded toward it.

Look across. Press and lean: the blue walks out the way you lean, by a steady ratio, never reaching the rim. go there moves home to where the blue points.

The thumb. Wheel, pinch, + − change it: a smaller thumb brings home's neighbourhood nearer, a larger one lifts you up.

Detail. Each point of the disc reads the map from the tile its footprint earns: streets under home, then coarser and coarser toward the horizon, a level of the map for each doubling of what a point covers.
