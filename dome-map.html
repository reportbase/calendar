Where you stand. One thumb above the map, looking down. The middle is straight down; the rim is the horizon all round, the whole world crowded toward it.

Move. Drag the map: the point you pressed stays under your finger, so a drag near the horizon carries you a long way. Let go moving and it coasts. Tap a place to fly there. The arrows glide a thumb a second.

The thumb. Wheel, pinch, + − change it: a smaller thumb brings home's neighbourhood nearer, a larger one lifts you up.

The lay. Out to one thumb (the gold ring, half the radius) the map is in proportion, undistorted. Past it each doubling of distance gets half the room of the last, so the whole world fits before the rim. Detail. Each point reads the map from the tile its footprint earns: streets under you, coarser toward the horizon.

Tap anywhere to close.
