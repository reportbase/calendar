Round is the note, out is the octave. The twelve notes go clockwise from the top. The gold ring, one thumb out, is the tonic's octave; each ring out is an octave higher, each ring in an octave lower, the middle the lowest.

Play. Press and hold anywhere: it sounds while held and follows your finger. Several fingers play a chord. Draw a finger round and outward and the pitch climbs without a step.

Endless sounds every octave at once, loudest in the middle, so a note is heard by its name alone: go round and round the spiral and it rises for ever, never arriving, the dial's horizon heard. Room gives every note the same area (the low notes room to play); width gives every octave the same width.

Chord (c): one finger plays a triad, a seventh or fifths built on the scale; turn it round the dome and it moves key. Equal / just: equal steps, or whole-number ratios to the tonic (3/2, 5/4 …). Rec (r) records what you play; press it again and it loops under you; clear stops it.

Snap holds each voice to the lit notes of the scale; off, it glides. Keys a s d f g h j k l play the scale up from the tonic, z x an octave down or up, space stops.

Tap anywhere to close.
