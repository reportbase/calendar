# Dials from an earlier session

Pages made in another session, added here unchanged. Each opens straight in a browser.

| Page | What it shows |
|---|---|
| `fisheye-sweep.html` | Situation 3: the reader sweeps from home in steps of h, in plain proportion to the corner (the working horizon), then one step per doubling toward a horizon no count reaches. No angle is used. |
| `breadth-sweep.html` | Known breadths with one, two, four or eight facings: the quarter circle, the semicircle, the circle and the sphere. g runs the radius from 1 (the unit circle) to the breadth B, with no situated observer. |
| `grow-or-slide.html` | Two things that reach a reader only as their two ends look alike. Tap one to follow its address back to the complete picture and see whether it grows evenly or from the middle out. |
| `pivot-and-sweep.html` | The reader is the pivot: a bar of 1 turns from pure horizontal (home) to pure vertical (the horizon). The readout gives g, v/h, the side and the octave; flip reads the pair the other way round, carry moves out one octave on its side. |
