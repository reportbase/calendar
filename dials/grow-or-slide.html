Grow or Slide

Two things, each reaching a reader only as its outline: its two ends, arriving moment by moment. Below each thing, its ends are kept over time, the newest at the top.

Both swing right and left. One of them stretches evenly, every part keeping its share of the length; the other grows and shrinks from its middle out, its two halves riding with its two ends. Their outlines are the same: both ends swinging, out of step. Their shapes differ, and are bumpy, and the ends do not care. The two ends carry correctly; which point is the thing's place is not in the outline.

Tap a thing to follow its address back to the complete picture: what the thing is, its own marks. Then the motion can be understood. Tap again to return to the outline. New pair shuffles which is which.
