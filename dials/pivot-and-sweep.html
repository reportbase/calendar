Pivot and Sweep

The reader is the pivot. A bar of 1 turns about it from pure horizontal to pure vertical, and the reader points along it. Home is where the sweep starts, the corner is where v equals h, the horizon is where it ends. Drag the bar's end, or sweep it from home in equal steps.
